First 100 Users Plan

Goal: get the first 100 genuine users without buying traffic.

Priority 1 — Personal network

  • Send the calculator to 20 trusted contacts who work in salaried jobs.
  • Ask each person to test their actual gross salary and share the result with one colleague.
  • Target: 30 users.

Priority 2 — Facebook

  • Publish 3 useful posts: “What is your take-home on UGX 1M?”, “UGX 2M?”, and “How much PAYE comes off your salary?”
  • Link only when the post genuinely answers a question; avoid spammy mass-posting.
  • Target: 30 users.

Priority 3 — LinkedIn

  • Publish a short salary-negotiation post using the reverse salary calculator.
  • Example angle: “If you want UGX 2M in your pocket, what gross salary should you negotiate?”
  • Target: 20 users.

Priority 4 — WhatsApp

  • Share one concise message with a direct calculator link.
  • Use status first; then send individually to people who are likely to find it useful.
  • Target: 20 users.

Rule

Do not buy ads until we know which message and page converts organic visitors into calculator users. Google recommends people-first, useful content rather than pages created mainly to manipulate rankings.

30-day measurement

  1. Users
  2. Calculator starts
  3. Completed calculations
  4. Most-used salary amounts
  5. Traffic source
  6. Top landing page
