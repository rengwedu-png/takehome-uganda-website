How we calculate

TakeHome Uganda separates the calculation into gross employment income, PAYE, employee NSSF and estimated take-home pay.

PAYE

The calculator contains two resident schedules: the PAYE schedule currently displayed on URA's online PAYE page and a separate FY2026/27 amendment schedule configured in the product. The user can see which schedule is active.

This distinction matters because URA's online PAYE page currently displays the legacy resident bands, while URA also publishes FY2026/27 tax-amendment material. We do not present the two as if they were the same source.

NSSF

Standard employee NSSF is modelled at 5% of gross employment income, with a 10% employer contribution. The calculator also provides special modes for cases where the standard employee deduction should not be applied.

What is excluded

  • Local Service Tax is not included in displayed take-home yet.
  • Individual payroll exemptions and special deductions are not fully modelled.
  • The reverse-salary tool does not model every possible payroll adjustment.

Sources

Primary tax references are Uganda Revenue Authority publications and NSSF Uganda guidance. Always confirm the applicable law and payroll treatment before relying on a result for compliance.
