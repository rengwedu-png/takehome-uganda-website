Why the new rates matter

The most useful number for an employee is not simply the PAYE deduction. It is the amount remaining after PAYE and applicable employee deductions such as NSSF.

TakeHome Uganda lets you calculate gross-to-net salary, work backwards from a desired take-home figure, and compare two job offers.

Important note

TakeHome Uganda is an independent salary-planning tool, not an official URA calculator. Tax rules and implementation guidance can change. For payroll compliance, confirm the applicable treatment with URA or your employer.
