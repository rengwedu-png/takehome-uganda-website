REVENUE MODEL

Three revenue engines

TakeHome Uganda should not depend on one income source.

1. Advertising

Best for broad free traffic. Revenue depends on page views, geography, ad demand and user engagement. Treat it as a volume business, not the only plan.

2. Premium reports

Best for higher revenue per engaged user. A small percentage of calculator users could pay for a polished salary negotiation or offer-comparison report.

3. B2B tools

Best long-term opportunity. Employers and HR/payroll teams have a stronger willingness to pay for budgeting, salary scenarios and bulk calculations than ordinary visitors.
