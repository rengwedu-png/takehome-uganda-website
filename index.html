Frequently asked questions

Is this an official URA calculator?

No. TakeHome Uganda is an independent planning tool. For payroll filing and compliance, confirm the applicable treatment with URA, your employer or a qualified adviser.

Does NSSF reduce PAYE?

The calculator treats employee NSSF as a separate deduction from gross pay rather than reducing the PAYE base, consistent with the model used here.

Does the calculator include LST?

Not in the displayed take-home yet. LST has timing and PAYE-interaction considerations, so we are keeping it separate until a month-specific model is implemented.

Are my salary details stored?

The calculator is designed to perform calculations in your browser without requiring an account. Do not enter information you would not want stored on any website.

Important

This is an independent estimate using the configured FY2026/27 PAYE rules and NSSF assumptions. It is not an official URA or NSSF calculator. URA publishes FY2026/27 tax-amendment material, while its ordinary PAYE rates page currently displays the legacy resident bands. TakeHome Uganda exposes both schedules so users can see which basis is being used.

PAYE is calculated before standard employee NSSF. NSSF treatment depends on eligibility; non-resident employees generally attract a special employer contribution rather than the standard employee 5% deduction. Local Service Tax (LST) is a local-government tax. Current guidance says it is payable by eligible employees earning above UGX 100,000, with annual bands up to UGX 100,000 and collection from employees in four instalments between July and October. LST is also deductible before PAYE, so simply subtracting an average monthly LST amount would not accurately reproduce payroll-month PAYE. We therefore do not include LST in the displayed take-home until we implement a month-specific LST/PAYE model. Exemptions and other special deductions are also not included.

Tax-source note: URA's FY2026/27 amendment material and its ordinary online PAYE page currently show different resident schedules. This calculator makes that distinction visible rather than hiding it. Check the latest official URA guidance before relying on a result for payroll.
