About TakeHome Uganda

Know what actually reaches your pocket.

TakeHome Uganda is an independent salary-planning website built to help people in Uganda understand the difference between gross pay and estimated take-home pay.

What you can do here

  • Estimate monthly take-home pay
  • Compare the configured PAYE schedules
  • Work backwards from a desired take-home amount
  • Compare two salary offers
  • Explore salary-specific examples

What it is not

TakeHome Uganda is not a government website and is not an official URA or NSSF calculator. Results are planning estimates and should be confirmed with your employer, URA, NSSF or a qualified adviser where necessary.
